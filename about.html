About

∃xProofAI studies the foundations of safe artificial agency.

Mission

∃xProofAI studies the foundations of safe artificial agency. We use formal tools from logic, epistemology, decision theory, game theory, and philosophy of AI to understand how advanced systems represent the world, form and pursue objectives, and can be made more interpretable and aligned.

The name

∃x is read “there exists an x.” In logic, an existence claim is settled by finding a witness: an object that satisfies the condition. Here the condition is being a route to safe AI. The name ∃xProofAI is pronounced “ExProof AI.”

Methods

Logic, epistemology, decision theory, game theory, and philosophy of AI.
