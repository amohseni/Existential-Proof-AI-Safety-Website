Research areas

Areas

Agency

How should we model artificial systems as agents with beliefs, preferences, and decision procedures?

Why it mattersMuch of AI safety depends on whether and when advanced systems can be meaningfully understood in agentic terms.

ProjectsEvolutionary Theory of Advanced AI AdaptationsA Bayesian Reduction of Causation

Open questions

  1. When does a decision theory describe a trained system well enough to predict how its behavior generalizes?
  2. Can models that are causally isolated from one another still coordinate?
  3. What is a world model, and what does a causal model do for the agent that has one?

Interpretability

What evidence warrants attributing beliefs, goals, or representations to advanced AI systems?

Why it mattersDeployment decisions will rest on what probes and evaluations report. A probe that tracks a correlate of truth, and not a belief, fails silently, and a silent failure that feeds a deployment decision is worse than no measurement.

ProjectsRadical AI InterpretabilityThe Robustness of Agential Representations

Open questions

  1. What would make a probe for deception or situational awareness a measurement of it?
  2. What does an evaluation show when the system can tell that it is being evaluated?
  3. Probes are validated against behavior and then used to check behavioral evaluations. Where does that regress stop?

Value

How should we think about value uncertainty, preference, and normative learning?

Why it mattersTraining pipelines already settle whose feedback counts and how conflicts are resolved, usually without stating these as choices.

ProjectsLearning the Value of Value Learning

Open questions

  1. Which aggregation rules do current training pipelines assume, and how should they be compared?
  2. How should a system act for one person who is uncertain about her own preferences, when expected preference is not well defined?

Alignment

How can humans safely delegate to increasingly capable systems whose objectives may diverge from their own?

Why it mattersPerfect alignment is unlikely, so the practical question is when imperfect alignment is good enough. A safety case for deployment needs a way to say how much alignment a given role requires.

ProjectsAI Alignment as a Principal–Agent Problem

Open questions

  1. How reliable must the measurement of a trait be for a given deployment decision?
  2. What is it for an agent to allow itself to be changed, as opposed to merely being changeable?
  3. Can a system share our values while reasoning by a different decision theory?

Methods

Logic, epistemology, decision theory, game theory, and philosophy of AI.
