Research

Research areas

Areas

Agency

How should we model artificial systems as agents with beliefs, preferences, and decision procedures?

Why it mattersMuch of AI safety depends on whether and when advanced systems can be meaningfully understood in agentic terms.

Related workRadical AI InterpretabilityAI Alignment as a Principal–Agent ProblemEvolutionary Theory of Advanced AI Adaptations

Interpretability

What evidence warrants attributing beliefs, goals, or representations to advanced AI systems?

Why it mattersClaims that an AI system wants or believes something drive much of the argument about AI risk. Those claims need standards of evidence.

Related workRadical AI Interpretability

Value

How should we think about value uncertainty, preference, and normative learning?

Why it mattersA system that learns what people value has to act before that learning is complete. How much to learn, and when learning is worth its cost, are decision problems.

Related workLearning the Value of Value Learning

Alignment

How can humans safely delegate to increasingly capable systems whose objectives may diverge from their own?

Why it mattersDelegating to a more capable agent is a principal–agent problem in which the principal cannot fully observe or evaluate what the agent does.

Related workAI Alignment as a Principal–Agent ProblemEvolutionary Theory of Advanced AI Adaptations

Methods

Which formal tools can make claims about AI safety precise enough to prove, refute, or test?

Why it mattersVague claims about agency, goals, deception, and power cannot be checked. Formal statements of the same claims can.

Logic, formal epistemology, Bayesian inference, decision theory, game theory, evolutionary game theory, and agent-based modeling.

Related workA Bayesian Reduction of CausationEvolutionary Theory of Advanced AI Adaptations
