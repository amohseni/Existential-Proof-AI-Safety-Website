Aydin Mohseni

Assistant Professor of Philosophy, Carnegie Mellon University
Core Member, Center for Conceptual Research on Safe AI (CoRe Safe AI)

Works on how scientific communities produce knowledge, and how agents represent the world, value outcomes, and act, with a focus on AI safety.

Daniel Herrmann

Assistant Professor of Philosophy, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
Core Faculty, UNC Philosophy, Politics, and Economics Program

Works on decision theory for agents who model themselves as part of the world, on how to tell what AI systems believe and want, and on when to delegate decisions to them.

danher@unc.edu
