Daniel Herrmann

Assistant Professor of Philosophy, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
Core Faculty, UNC Philosophy, Politics, and Economics Program

A decision theorist and formal epistemologist. Asks when an AI system has beliefs and goals, how we could measure them, and when we should delegate a decision to a system whose values differ from ours.
