AI safety research

We are searching for a path to safe AI, if one exists.

We use logic, epistemology, decision theory, and mathematical modeling to understand advanced AI systems as agents, and to search for principled routes to interpretability, alignment, and safety.

Aydin Mohseni · Carnegie Mellon University
Daniel Herrmann · University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

Research

Agency

What does it mean to model an artificial system as an agent?

Interpretability

What evidence warrants attributing beliefs, goals, or representations to advanced AI systems?

Value

How should we think about value uncertainty, preference, and normative learning?

Alignment

How can humans safely delegate to increasingly capable systems whose objectives may diverge from their own?
